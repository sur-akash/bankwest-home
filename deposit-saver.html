The day it settles

Your Saver becomes your offset

The awkward moment in most deposit accounts is the end of them: the money leaves, the account closes, and you start again from zero somewhere else. Here, whatever's left after settlement stays put and turns into your free 100% Offset Transaction Account — same account, same app, now cutting interest instead of earning it.

  • No new account to open, no forms at settlement
  • $0 monthly fee — it's the included offset on your Simple Home Loan
  • Roundups you set up while saving keep running, now against the mortgage
  • Your other Bankwest savings link to it the same day, if you want them to
