When we'd insist on a specialist

Not because online can't take the form, but because these go wrong quietly if nobody's watching.

  • Construction and progress draw-downs
  • Bridging finance and peak-debt structuring
  • Trust, company and SMSF borrowers
  • Guarantor arrangements
  • Self-employed with under two years of returns
  • Rural, commercial-zoned or unusual security

Things to consider

The online application path is available for eligible applicants and property types. Applications involving construction, bridging finance, guarantors, trust, company or SMSF structures, or where a full valuation is required, are referred to a home loan specialist. You can transfer an online application to a specialist at any point without re-entering your details. Automated valuations are indicative and we may require a full valuation. Submitting an application involves a credit check, which is recorded on your credit file. Due to state laws, restrictions apply to digitally signing the Mortgage Form in some states and territories; you'll need a device with internet and email access, and a computer or tablet is recommended. Some exclusions apply. At settlement we open the included Offset Transaction Account and link eligible Bankwest deposit accounts held in the same name as the loan; you'll be shown the accounts to be linked before settlement and can exclude any of them. Lending and eligibility criteria, fees and charges apply. Rates are subject to change.
