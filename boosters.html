Three habits, none of which you have to remember

Roundups

Every card purchase rounds up to the next dollar, or the next $5 if you want it to bite. The difference sweeps to your offset overnight in one transfer, so your statement doesn't fill up with cents.

  • Skips the sweep if your account would go under
  • Pause it for a month without switching it off

Payment Splits

Set a percentage or a flat amount and we carve it off the moment your pay arrives. Money you never see in your spending balance is money you don't have to have discipline about.

  • Follows your pay cycle, whatever it is
  • Split across several offsets if you hold more than one

Rate Keeper

Rate cuts usually vanish into a slightly smaller direct debit and you never notice the money. Rate Keeper holds your repayment where it was and sends the difference to the offset instead.

  • We tell you before it starts, every time
  • One tap to take the cut in cash instead
Why the offset, not extra repayments

Same interest saved. Very different flexibility.

A dollar in a 100% offset reduces the balance you're charged interest on by exactly one dollar — identical to paying it off the loan. The difference is what happens when you need it back. Redraw is a request. Your offset is a debit card.

That's the whole reason a monthly fee on an offset account was worth arguing about, and the whole reason your first one is now free.

Roundups, Payment Splits and Rate Keeper move your own money between your own accounts. Funds moved to an Offset Transaction Account reduce the balance interest is charged on but do not reduce your loan balance or your minimum repayment, and remain available to you. Transfers are skipped where the source account has insufficient funds. Boosters can be paused or switched off at any time in the app. Projections on this page assume the rate, repayment and booster amounts you enter continue unchanged for the life of the loan, that all sweeps succeed, and that no redraws or withdrawals are made from the offset — real outcomes will differ. Credit interest is not payable on an Offset Transaction Account. Lending criteria, fees and charges apply.
