New — Repayment Boosters

Small money, pointed at your mortgage

Your everyday account already sees every coffee and every payday. Boosters turn that traffic into offset balance automatically, so paying down your loan stops being something you have to remember to do.

Roundups

Every card purchase rounds up to the next dollar — or the next $5 if you're feeling brave — and the difference lands in your offset that night.

Payment Splits

Carve a slice off every pay the moment it lands — a percentage or a flat amount — before it becomes money you were going to spend anyway.

Rate Keeper

When your variable rate drops, we hold your repayment where it was and send the difference to the offset — unless you tell us not to.

Common questions

Generally 20% of the property value if you want to avoid Lenders' Mortgage Insurance, though we lend above that with LMI. Home Deposit Saver shows you both lines — the 20% mark and the smaller deposit you could buy at — along with the LMI premium you'd pay for going early.

A variable rate moves with the market, so your repayments can go up or down, and you get full flexibility — unlimited extra repayments, redraw and a 100% offset. A fixed rate holds steady for the term you choose, which makes budgeting easier, but caps extra repayments at $10,000 a year and limits offset to 40%.

Photo ID, proof of income, and details of your expenses, debts and assets. If you already bank with us, Home Zone assembles most of that from your accounts, so an online application usually starts around 80% complete.

Your first 100% Offset Transaction Account on a Simple Home Loan has no monthly fee, and no annual or package fee is charged to get it. Additional offset accounts are $10 a month each — at three or more, the Complete Home Loan Package at $395 a year is usually cheaper, and we'll say so in the app. Credit interest isn't payable on an offset account.

Often, yes. Usable equity is roughly 80% of your property's current value less what you still owe. A specialist can walk through how much of it you could access and what it does to your repayments.

Owner-occupier loans are for a property you live in or intend to live in. Investor loans are for property bought predominantly to invest. Rates differ between the two, and refinancing an investor loan stays an investor loan.

Principal and interest repayments chip away at what you borrowed as well as the interest, so the balance falls. Interest-only repayments cover just the interest for a set period — lower repayments now, a larger balance later, and more interest over the life of the loan.

Your outgoing lender may charge a discharge fee, and government registration fees apply. On our side a $350 settlement fee applies where we take a new security; there's no application fee, and no monthly offset fee on your first offset.

Possibly. Two years of tax returns is the standard, but a specialist can look at a shorter trading history alongside prior experience in the same field, business account inflows and your overall position.

On a variable loan we'll write to you before the change takes effect and your minimum repayment adjusts. If the rate falls, Rate Keeper can hold your repayment at the old amount and send the difference to your offset — you can switch that off any time.

It's a tool that can help you identify the truer cost of a loan. It's calculated using a standard formula that includes the interest rate, as well as certain fees and charges relating to a loan (not all fees and charges are included).

Comparison rate warning. Comparison rate is calculated on the statutory assumption of a $150,000 loan over 25 years, but the minimum required loan amount is $200,000 for the Complete Home Loan Package. Different rates apply for different loan amounts and may depend on the duration of a fixed rate period or the ratio of the loan amount to the property value. WARNING: This comparison rate applies only to the example or examples given. Different amounts and terms will result in different comparison rates. Costs such as redraw fees or early repayment fees, and cost savings such as fee waivers, are not included in the comparison rate but may influence the cost of the loan.

Offset Transaction Accounts must be held in the same name as the associated loan account. Credit interest is not payable on the Offset Transaction Account.

One 100% Offset Transaction Account is included with each Simple Home Loan at no monthly fee. Additional Offset Transaction Accounts are charged at $10 per month per account, charged to each offset account held, not the home loan.

With our Complete Fixed and Fixed Rate home loans a maximum 40% offset is available. Other exclusions apply. For the Fixed Rate Home Loan no offset fee applies during the fixed term, however a $12 monthly loan maintenance fee applies. At the end of the fixed term the loan moves to the included free offset tier, and additional offsets are $10 per month each.

A Home Zone estimate is an indication of what we might be able to lend, generated from transaction data on your Bankwest accounts over the preceding 12 months. It isn't an offer of credit, a pre-approval or a conditional approval, and it's produced without validation or full assessment of your circumstances. Serviceability is assessed at a buffered rate above the product rate in line with responsible lending obligations. Income and expenses held at other institutions won't be visible to us unless you share them.

Projected dates are estimates based on the deposit amount, contribution rate and interest rate you enter or that we observe, and assume those continue unchanged. They don't account for property price movement, changes to duty concessions, or changes in your circumstances. Stamp duty and LMI figures shown are indicative and calculated on illustrative schedules; the amounts you actually pay are set by your state or territory revenue office and by the insurer. Interest rates on savings accounts are variable.

At settlement we open the included Offset Transaction Account and link eligible Bankwest deposit accounts held in the same name as the loan. You'll be shown the accounts to be linked before settlement and can exclude any of them, or unlink them afterwards in the app. Accounts held jointly with someone who isn't a borrower, and accounts with different account holders, aren't eligible for automatic linking. Credit interest is not payable on funds held in an offset.

Roundups, Payment Splits and Rate Keeper move your own money between your own accounts. Funds moved to an Offset Transaction Account reduce the balance interest is charged on but do not reduce your loan balance or your minimum repayment, and remain available to you. Transfers are skipped where the source account has insufficient funds. Boosters can be paused or switched off at any time in the app.

The online application path is available for eligible applicants and property types. Applications involving construction, bridging finance, guarantors, trust or company structures, or where a full valuation is required, are referred to a home loan specialist. You can transfer an online application to a specialist at any point without re-entering your details. Due to state laws, restrictions apply to digitally signing the Mortgage Form in some states and territories; you'll need a device with internet and email access, and a computer or tablet is recommended. Some exclusions apply.

Pre-approval, or 'approved in principle', is a letter that indicates the amount we might be able to lend to you. It's based on the information you've given us (without validation or assessment) and is issued after we perform a credit check. This is different from a conditional approval, where your information is validated and assessed before it's issued.

When you buy a property, other fees may apply, including government and bank fees. A $350 settlement (legal disbursement) fee applies when we take a new security, and progress payments are $80 each outside the Complete Package. Rates and fees are subject to change. A full list of fees will be set out in your home loan contract. Lenders' Mortgage Insurance (LMI) protects the lender, not you, and can be avoided by saving 20% or more of the property value.
