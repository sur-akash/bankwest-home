How Home Zone builds the number

A borrowing power calculator asks you to estimate your own spending, then believes you. An assessor won't. Home Zone does what the assessor does.

Find the income that's really recurring

Salary credits are matched by employer and frequency. Bonuses and one-off deposits are shaded out. Savings interest is counted at 80%, the way it would be in an assessment.

Separate habits from events

Twelve months is long enough to see that the flights were a holiday and the groceries are a life. Single-occurrence spend is excluded and shown to you, so you can put it back if we got it wrong.

Apply the benchmark floor

We assess at the higher of what you actually spend and the household expenditure benchmark for your circumstances. If you're already at the floor, cutting your spending won't lift your estimate — and we'll say so rather than let you chase it.

Assess at a buffered rate

Your surplus is tested against repayments at 3.00% above the product rate, so the estimate holds up if rates move. That buffer is why the number is lower than a repayment calculator would suggest — and why it's worth something.

Keep it, and reuse it

Your Zone stays live. Come back when you've found a property, add the address, and the application starts already filled in.

No credit check

A Zone estimate leaves no mark on your credit file. Nothing is reported, and you can look as often as you like.

Your own data, nothing new

Home Zone reads accounts you already hold with us. It doesn't pull in outside accounts unless you connect them yourself.

An estimate, and we say so

This isn't pre-approval or an offer of credit. It's the same arithmetic an assessor runs, on data we already hold — which is why it tends to land close.

Not loan-ready yet?

If the number isn't there, the deposit is usually the reason. Home Deposit Saver tells you the month it will be.

Things to consider

A Home Zone estimate is an indication of what we might be able to lend, generated from transaction data on your Bankwest accounts over the preceding 12 months. It isn't an offer of credit, a pre-approval or a conditional approval, and it's produced without validation or full assessment of your circumstances. Serviceability is assessed at a buffered rate above the product rate in line with responsible lending obligations. Income and expenses held at other institutions won't be visible to us unless you share them. Household expenditure benchmarks, credit card assessment rates and LMI premiums shown here are illustrative. Lending criteria, fees and charges apply. Rates are subject to change.
